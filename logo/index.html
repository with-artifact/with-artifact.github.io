The WA mark, taken apart

The original mark, the one used before #27, is cropped from "WA" set in Inter Bold with Inter's own kerning, at 2048 units per em. I shaped the word with HarfBuzz against Inter 4.1 and matched the outlines point by point. The slash is the W's last stroke and the Λ is the A with its crossbar cut out. The whole crop is shifted 1341 units left.

Logo system · three versions

The W is Inter Bold's own outline. Only its last stroke changes: it becomes the parallel bar, leaned to the A's 18.86°. The W's third stroke ends where the bar's edge crosses the shared foot, along Inter's own curve. Each version is a crop or a recolor of the same drawing, and the primary mark sits in the same coordinates in all three.
