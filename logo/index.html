The WA monogram

Inter Bold "WA" with two strokes taken out, one from the W and the A's crossbar, then every stroke aligned to the A's angles. What's left is a W and an A that share one rhythm.

How it's made

01Set "WA" in Inter Bold

Inter's own outlines and its own W–A spacing.

→
02Take out two strokes

The W's third stroke and the A's crossbar, shown in red.

→
03Align to the A

The V is the A turned upside down. The slash is the A's left leg as an even bar, at the same 18.86° and the same 337 width. Every top space is 352.

Geometry

1Source shape: Inter Bold's A without its crossbar
337Slash width, the A's leg at the baseline
18.86°Every /, the A's left leg
19.31°Every \, the A's right leg
352Both top spaces, constant to the baseline
